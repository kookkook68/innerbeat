Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026 · Developer: Lee Hyeyeon, Seoul, South Korea · Contact: innerbeat.app@gmail.com

The short version

  • Innerbeat works entirely on your device. Your readings, heart rhythm data, tags and breathing sessions are stored on your iPhone only.
  • We do not run servers that receive your health data. There is no account, no sign-in and no email collection inside the app.
  • We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising. The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Camera frames are processed in memory to detect your pulse and are never saved or transmitted. No photo or video is stored.

What the app processes

Camera brightness. While you measure, the app reads the average brightness of camera frames to detect heartbeats. Frames are discarded immediately after this calculation.

Heart rate and HRV results. Beat-to-beat intervals, heart rate, HRV (RMSSD) and session summaries are saved on your device so the app can show trends and your personal baseline.

Optional tags. If you tag a reading (sleep, alcohol, workout, caffeine, feeling sick), that tag is saved on your device.

Morning reminder (optional). If you turn it on, the reminder is scheduled as a local notification on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Apple Health. The current version does not read from or write to Apple Health. If a future version adds this, it will be optional and this policy will be updated first.

What we receive

Purchases. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. We receive anonymised transaction information from Apple to unlock Pro features; we never see your payment details.

Crash and diagnostics. If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymised crash reports. These contain no health data.

Support email. If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and delete it afterwards on request.

Your data, your control

  • Pro users can export their readings as a CSV file from Settings and share it wherever they choose.
  • Delete a single reading by swiping it in History.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app. Nothing remains elsewhere.
  • If you use iCloud device backup, your app data is included in that backup under Apple's iCloud terms. You can exclude it in iOS settings.

Children

Innerbeat is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Not a medical device

Innerbeat is a general wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition and is not a substitute for medical advice. If you have a heart condition or health concern, talk to a healthcare professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and material changes will be noted in the app.
