How it works

Three steps, one finger, no setup.

1. Cover the camera

Put a fingertip over the rear lens. The flash lights your finger and the camera sees the tiny brightness change of every heartbeat.

2. Read for 60 seconds

Innerbeat measures beat-to-beat timing and computes your HRV, then compares it with your own 7-day baseline.

3. Breathe and watch

Follow the circle at six breaths a minute. Your heart rhythm starts to swing with your breath on screen, in real time.

Honest about accuracy

We checked Innerbeat against Apple Watch heartbeat timing in 7 side-by-side recordings (one person, Nov 2026).

±2.3 bpm

Heart rate: within 2.3 bpm in all 7 recordings

±10%

HRV with normal breathing: within 10% in all 3 recordings

Heart rate only

HRV can be far off while you breathe slowly, so breathing sessions compare heart rate, not HRV. (Within 11% in 2 of 4 slow-breathing recordings, but off by 17% and 100% in the other 2.)

Movement, pressing hard or a loose finger lower accuracy. Innerbeat drops those beats and tells you why. It is a wellness tool, not a medical device.
